How it works

  1. Clempo collects news from many sources each day and removes repeated stories.
  2. It keeps the stories that matter most to the world that day. The number changes from day to day.
  3. It rewrites each story in neutral language that is suitable for kids.
  4. An editor reads and approves every story before it is published.

The list ends when the day's stories end. There is no feed to scroll and nothing to keep you on the page.

Privacy

  • No cookies
  • No sign-up, and no personal details asked for

Clempo does not collect information about the people who read it.
