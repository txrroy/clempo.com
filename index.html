How it works

  1. Clempo collects news from many sources each day and removes duplicates.
  2. It ranks the stories by how much they affect the world, and keeps the top 20.
  3. It rewrites each story in neutral language that is suitable for kids.
  4. An editor reads and approves every story before it is published.

Why I built it

When I was a child in India in the 1980s, my dad had me watch a 5-minute English news bulletin on TV every day. Today, my kids get most of their news from social media. I built Clempo to give them a short daily update they can finish.

Apps

Apps for iPhone and Android are in progress. Until they are ready, Clempo works in the browser on any phone. There is nothing to install.
