01How it works

A short list, checked by a person.

  1. Clempo collects news from many sources each day and removes repeated stories.
  2. It keeps the stories that matter most to the world that day. The number changes from day to day.
  3. It rewrites each story in neutral language that is suitable for kids.
  4. An editor reads and approves every story before it is published.

02Privacy

No ads, no trackers, no cookies.

Clempo does not show ads or use analytics, and it does not set cookies. There is no sign-up, and it never asks for personal details. Clempo does not collect information about the people who read it.

03The idea

News your family can finish together.

Clempo is a short daily list of world news that parents and kids can read side by side. When the list ends, you are done for the day. There is no feed to come back to.
